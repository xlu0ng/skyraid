Sky Raid Arena

Privacy Policy
Last updated: 10 Nov 2026

Sky Raid Arena ("the game") is made by Lại Xuân Lương ("the developer"). Contact: laixuannam925@gmail.com.

Data on your device

The game keeps your progress (scores, unlocked ships, upgrades, settings and a play log) on your device. Single-player modes do not send this data to the developer, and the game has no account or sign-in of its own. The data that leaves your device is what the advertising SDK described below collects, and what ARENA online sends when you choose to play it (see "ARENA online" below).

Play log

The play log is copied only when you press the "COPY PLAY LOG" button, and it leaves your device only if you choose to send it to the developer yourself.

Purchases and Google services

If you buy in-game credits or items, the purchase is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment details. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress may be saved to your Google account's cloud save; that data is processed by Google under Google's policy, and the developer cannot read it.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example to revive or to receive extra supplies. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization at any time in your Android settings.

ARENA online

When you play ARENA with other people, the game connects to the developer's server. The server receives your IP address (part of any network connection), a pilot name generated by the game (for example "Pilot1234"), your selected ship and skin, and your in-match controls (move direction, fire, revive request). This data is processed in memory only while the match runs and is discarded when the match ends or you leave. The developer does not store IP addresses, names or online match history. The hosting provider may keep network-level connection logs under its own policy. Match traffic is not encrypted. No account is needed, and the game has no chat or other text written by players. If no other players are available, bots fill the empty seats.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to delete the data stored on your device. To delete a cloud save, remove the Saved Games for this game in the Google Play Games settings.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
